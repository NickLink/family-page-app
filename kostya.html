3-Day Muscle Growth Program

Your interactive guide to a knee-friendly hypertrophy plan.

Important Principles for Success

This section outlines the core concepts crucial for maximizing muscle growth and ensuring safety. Understanding and applying these principles consistently will be the foundation of your progress.

Progressive Overload

The key to muscle growth. Aim to gradually increase the weight you lift over time. Once you can comfortably perform the upper end of the rep range with good form, increase the weight slightly in your next session.

Mind-Muscle Connection

Don't just lift the weight. Focus on squeezing the target muscle with every repetition. This enhances muscle activation and growth.

Proper Form

Always prioritize correct form over lifting heavy. This prevents injury, especially with your knee concerns, and ensures the right muscles are being worked.

Rest & Nutrition

Muscles grow when you rest. Get 7-9 hours of quality sleep. To build muscle, eat enough protein (1.6-2.2g per kg of body weight) and be in a slight calorie surplus.

Sports Supplements

While not necessary, some supplements are well-researched and can safely support your goals. Always consult a doctor before starting any new supplement.

Creatine Monohydrate

The most studied supplement for performance. It helps your muscles produce energy during heavy lifting, leading to increased strength and muscle mass over time.

Dosage: Take 3-5 grams daily. There is no need for a "loading phase." Mix it with water, juice, or your protein shake. Timing is not critical, but consistency is key.

Day 1: Push Day

This workout focuses on the pushing muscles of the upper body: chest, shoulders, and triceps. The volume is balanced across these groups to promote proportional growth.

Incline Dumbbell Press: 4 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Lie on an incline bench (30-45 degrees). Hold dumbbells at chest level, palms forward. Press up until your arms are extended but not locked. Lower slowly. Watch Video

Flat Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Lie on a flat bench. Grip the bar slightly wider than your shoulders. Lower it to your mid-chest, keeping elbows at a 45-degree angle. Press powerfully back up. Watch Video

Cable Crossover: 3 sets, 10-15 reps▼

Set pulleys high. Stand in the middle, grab handles, and step forward. With elbows slightly bent, pull handles down and across your body until they meet. Squeeze your chest, then return slowly. Watch Video

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 4 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Sit on a bench with back support. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward. Press weights overhead until arms are fully extended. Lower slowly. Watch Video

Dumbbell Lateral Raise: 3 sets, 12-15 reps▼

Stand with dumbbells at your sides. With a slight bend in your elbows, raise weights out to your sides to shoulder level. Lead with your elbows and lower with control. Watch Video

Triceps Pushdown (Rope): 4 sets, 10-15 reps▼

Attach a rope to a high pulley. Keep elbows pinned to your sides and push the rope down until arms are fully extended, separating the rope ends. Return slowly. Watch Video

Overhead Dumbbell Extension: 3 sets, 10-12 reps▼

Sit or stand holding one dumbbell with both hands overhead. Lower it behind your head by bending your elbows. Extend your arms to raise it back to the start. Watch Video

Day 2: Pull Day

This session targets the pulling muscles: the entire back and biceps. The focus is on building width and thickness in the back, complemented by direct arm work.

Pull-Ups (or Lat Pulldown): 4 sets, 6-10 reps▼

Pull-ups: Grab bar, hang, and pull chest to bar. Lat Pulldowns: Sit, secure knees, pull bar to upper chest. Squeeze back muscles on both. Watch Video

Barbell Bent-Over Row: 4 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Hinge at your hips with a straight back. Pull the barbell towards your lower chest, squeezing your back muscles. Lower under control. Watch Video

Seated Cable Row: 3 sets, 10-12 reps▼

Sit with chest up and back straight. Pull the handle towards your abdomen, squeezing your shoulder blades together. Slowly extend your arms. Watch Video

Face Pulls: 3 sets, 15-20 reps▼

Set a rope on a cable machine at eye level. Pull the rope towards your face, aiming to get your hands by your ears. Squeeze your rear shoulders. Watch Video

Barbell Bicep Curl: 4 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Stand with elbows pinned to your sides. Curl the barbell up towards your shoulders without swinging. Squeeze your biceps at the top and lower slowly. Watch Video

Incline Dumbbell Curl: 3 sets, 10-12 reps▼

Sit on an incline bench. Let arms hang straight down. Curl the dumbbells up, rotating wrists so palms face shoulders at the top. Lower slowly for a full stretch. Watch Video

Hammer Curl: 3 sets, 10-12 reps▼

Stand holding dumbbells with a neutral grip (palms facing in). Keep elbows at your sides and curl the weights up towards your shoulders. Lower with control. Watch Video

Day 3: Full Body & Legs

This workout stimulates muscles throughout the body, with a special emphasis on knee-friendly leg exercises to build a strong foundation. Upper body exercises are included to increase weekly training frequency.

Leg Press: 4 sets, 10-15 reps▼

Sit with feet shoulder-width apart on the platform. Lower the weight until knees are at a 90-degree angle. Press back up, but do not lock your knees. Watch Video

Romanian Deadlift (Dumbbell): 4 sets, 10-12 reps▼

Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs. Hinge at your hips with a slight knee bend and straight back. Lower the weights, then return by squeezing glutes. Watch Video

Leg Extension Machine: 3 sets, 12-15 reps▼

Sit on the machine, shins against the pad. Extend your legs until they are straight, squeezing your quads at the top. Lower with control. Watch Video

Seated Hamstring Curl: 3 sets, 12-15 reps▼

Position the pad above your ankles. Curl your legs down and back as far as possible, squeezing your hamstrings. Return slowly to the start. Watch Video

Seated Calf Raise: 4 sets, 15-20 reps▼

Place the balls of your feet on the platform. Lower your heels for a stretch, then press up onto your toes as high as possible, squeezing your calves. Watch Video

Dumbbell Bench Press: 3 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells at your chest. Press the weights up until your arms are fully extended. Lower slowly to the sides of your chest. Watch Video

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row: 3 sets, 8-12 reps▼

Place one knee and hand on a bench. Hold a dumbbell in the opposite hand and pull it towards your hip, keeping your back straight. Lower with control. Watch Video

Arnold Press: 3 sets, 10-12 reps▼

Sit holding dumbbells in front of shoulders, palms facing you. As you press overhead, rotate your hands so palms face forward at the top. Reverse the motion on the way down. Watch Video
